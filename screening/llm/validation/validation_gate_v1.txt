QWEN3:4B VALIDATION GATE - v1

Defined before inspection of validation outputs.

PURPOSE

Evaluate whether Qwen3:4B with the criterion-based screening
architecture v1.2 is sufficiently conservative to be used as
decision support during title/abstract screening.

PASS CONDITIONS

G1 - STRUCTURAL VALIDITY

All 30 validation records must produce structurally valid outputs.

G2 - PROTECTION OF HUMAN INCLUDES

Human include -> Qwen exclude = 0.

No record classified as include in the reference decisions may be
suggested as exclude by Qwen.

G3 - RETENTION SENSITIVITY

Retention sensitivity >= 0.90.

Human retained =
human_decision in {include, maybe}

Qwen retained =
llm_decision in {include, maybe}

Retention sensitivity =
number of human-retained records not classified as exclude by Qwen
divided by all human-retained records.

DESCRIPTIVE METRICS

The following metrics will also be reported:

- exact three-class agreement;
- Cohen's kappa;
- confusion matrix;
- human maybe -> Qwen exclude;
- agreement on exclusion codes when both classify exclude.

DECISION RULE

PASS only if G1, G2 and G3 are all satisfied.

The objective of the LLM is decision support with emphasis on
screening sensitivity, not autonomous exclusion.

No record will be excluded automatically based solely on Qwen output.

If the gate fails, these same 30 validation records must not be used
to optimize a revised prompt/model and subsequently be presented as
an independent validation of that revised version.
